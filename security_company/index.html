Manned Guarding Services

Our manned guarding services form the frontline of our security solutions. We deploy highly trained, vetted, and disciplined security officers who prevent, detect, and respond to threats at your premises — from residential estates to construction sites and government institutions.

What We Offer:

  • Visitor Logs & Written Reports: Guards record entries, incidents, and handover notes clearly and consistently.
  • 24/7 Gate & Access Control: Strict visitor screening, vehicle logs, and badge scanning.
  • Perimeter Patrols: Regular, scheduled, and random foot patrols with digital patrol logs.
  • Emergency Incident Reporting: Structured reporting of occurrences, safety issues, or security breaches.
  • Residential & Commercial Guarding: Custom protective strategies for estates, offices, schools, and construction sites.

All our guards undergo regular supervision and continuous training in customer service, self-defense, fire safety, and emergency response protocols.

Temporary Guarding & Event Security

We provide temporary event security and short-term crowd management for weddings, corporate functions, religious gatherings, exhibitions, and entertainment events. Our officers manage scanning, access control, and crowd flow so your event runs safely and smoothly.

What We Offer:

  • Crowd Dynamics & Management: Professional handling of high-capacity crowds with custom barriers and zoning.
  • Gate Access & Scanning: Systematic guest verification, metal detector scanning, and bag inspection.
  • VIP Protection & Ushering: Discreet personal security escorts for corporate VIPs, executives, or celebrities.
  • Parking & Traffic Coordination: Organised vehicle direction, parking allocation, and perimeter security.

We work closely with event organisers and local law enforcement to map out emergency exits, risk zones, and response pathways prior to the event.

Electronic Security Systems

We integrate physical guarding with modern technology — CCTV, alarms, electric fencing, biometrics, and access control. Our technicians install, configure, and maintain systems tailored to your property, with optional linkage to our rapid response network.

What We Offer:

  • CCTV Installation & Monitoring: HD & 4K security camera installations with remote mobile viewing and AI motion detection.
  • Electric Fence Installation: Professional installation of high-tension fences with alarm triggers linked to monitoring and response.
  • Electric Fencing Maintenance: Regular inspections and repairs to keep perimeter systems reliable year-round.
  • Biometric Access Control: Smart biometric fingerprint/face scanners and card access gates for sensitive office areas.
  • Intruder Alarms: Motion detectors, door contacts, panic buttons, and central alarm control panels.

We offer full system audits, expert planning, clean installations, and regular maintenance packages to ensure 100% uptime.

Dog Patrol & K9 Services

Our dog patrol and K9 services add a strong deterrent layer for warehouses, industrial sites, construction yards, and large properties. Trained handlers and disciplined dogs work together on scheduled and random patrols.

What We Offer:

  • Perimeter Boundary Patrols: High-deterrence K9 patrol around large industrial properties, construction yards, and warehouse complexes.
  • Scent & Threat Detection: Specialized training for detecting intruders, contraband, or unauthorized items.
  • Trained Guard Dogs: Disciplined, healthy dogs that follow strict command responses from their handler.
  • Rapid Action Teams: Mobile response teams equipped with K9 cages for immediate deployment.

Our dogs undergo rigorous health checks and continuous field training, ensuring they operate ethically and effectively.

Security Consultancy & Risk Assessment

Our consultancy team audits your site, reviews procedures, and designs practical security plans — combining manpower, technology, and policy so you invest where it matters most.

What We Offer:

  • Vulnerability Audits: Complete site inspections to identify weak entry points, blind spots, and procedural flaws.
  • Security Layout Design: Custom placement planning for cameras, security guards, sensors, and lighting.
  • Disaster & Crisis Management: Formulating emergency evacuation, fire hazard response, and lockdown procedures.
  • Compliance & Regulation Audits: Aligning company security protocols with Kenyan private security regulatory frameworks.

Stay ahead of potential risks by implementing proactive structural policies that secure your business continuity.

Rapid Response & Rescue Services

When an emergency happens, seconds count. Our rapid response and rescue teams serve Mombasa, Nairobi, Malindi, Kilifi, and Kwale — responding to alarms, panic signals, and distress calls with trained mobile units.

What We Offer:

  • 24/7 Dispatch Control: Constant monitoring of alarm transmitters linked to our central dispatch center.
  • Mobile Response Units: GPS-tracked vehicles manned by highly trained, tactical response guards.
  • Crisis Evacuation: Providing safe and quick escort support during localized riots, fires, or medical crises.
  • Authority Coordination: Instant linkage to local police departments, fire engines, and medical ambulances.

Get immediate peace of mind by linking your home or business alarm transmitter to our rapid response network.
